Trust and safety

Transparent, independent, and simple.

SEE 2083 is made as a study support platform. It is not an official government or examination board website.

Independent study tool

SEE 2083 is not affiliated with NEB, CDC, MoEST, or any official government exam body. Students should always follow official notices and school instructions.

No login required

Students can use the website without creating an account. Bookmarks and theme preference are stored only in the student’s browser.

Content review process

Study materials are added chapter by chapter. If a mistake is found, students and teachers can report it so the content can be corrected.

Contact and updates

Join, watch, or report a mistake.

Use the community and video links for updates. If you find a wrong answer, broken link, or confusing explanation, report it so it can be improved.
