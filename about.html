About SEE 2083

SEE 2083 is an independent Grade 10 study platform for students in Nepal. It helps students study chapter by chapter through notes, practice questions, mock tests, visual resources, search, and bookmarks.

Content growth policy

Trust and disclaimer

Independent and transparent

SEE 2083 is an independent study platform. It is not an official website of NEB, CDC, MoEST, or any government body. Students should always follow official notices, school instructions, and official textbooks.

If mistakes are found, they can be reported for correction so the platform can keep improving for students, parents, and teachers.

Privacy and safety

No login, no personal data

SEE 2083 can be used without creating an account. The website does not collect student personal data. Bookmarks and theme preferences are stored in the student's own browser, so clearing browser data may remove saved bookmarks.
