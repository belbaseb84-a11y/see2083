# Review Workflow

Use review status to keep SEE2083 content safe and organized.

## Status Values

- `draft` means AI-generated, raw, or not reviewed yet.
- `checked` means reviewed by a person or teacher.
- `published` means visible on the website.
- `rejected` means not usable.

## Suggested Flow

1. Add new content as `draft`.
2. Review facts, spelling, syllabus match, and formatting.
3. Mark content as `checked`.
4. Do a final quality pass.
5. Mark content as `published`.

## Important

Do not publish copied textbook text without permission.

Use your own explanations, summaries, and practice questions after review.

## Validator Gate

Content should not be marked `published` until the relevant validator passes.

Use this simple flow:

1. Keep new or AI-assisted content as `draft`.
2. Review it with a person or teacher.
3. Mark it as `checked`.
4. Run the validators.
5. Mark it as `published` only after the checks pass.
